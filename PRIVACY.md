# Privacy Policy

## What We Collect

When you click "Submit Results", we collect:

- **Browser & OS**: User agent string, platform
- **Display capabilities**: HDR support, color gamut (P3/Rec.2020), color depth
- **Screen dimensions**: In CSS and native pixels, device pixel ratio
- **Viewport size**: Browser window dimensions
- **WebGPU info**: Availability, GPU vendor (if exposed)
- **Image codec support**: WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, HEIC
- **Network hints**: Connection type, downlink estimate, data saver mode
- **Device hints**: Memory, CPU cores, touch capability
- **Preferences**: Color scheme, reduced motion, contrast, orientation
- **Test results**: Your HDR headroom and color gamut test responses

## What We Don't Collect

- IP addresses (not logged by our spreadsheet)
- Cookies or tracking identifiers
- Personal information
- Browsing history

## Why We Collect This

This data helps us understand real-world browser capabilities for HDR and wide color gamut content. It's used for research into responsive image delivery and display feature detection.

## Privacy Notes

Many of the device/network hints may be restricted, spoofed, or unavailable due to browser privacy protections. We collect what's available but don't rely on accuracy.

## Data Storage

Results are stored in a Google Sheet. The raw data is not published, but aggregate findings may be shared publicly.

## Opt Out

Don't click "Submit Results" if you don't want to share data. The tests work fine without submitting.
